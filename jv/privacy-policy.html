Privacy Policy & Data Protection

GDPR, CCPA & International Privacy Standards for BotifyAI

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

BotifyAI (operated by FinxHost Ltd, "Company", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to safeguarding your personal data and respecting your privacy rights. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and protect personal information when you use our website, mobile apps, and customer messaging platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account & Contact Details: Full name, email address, billing information, and account credentials provided during purchase via JVZoo.
  • Messaging & Channel Configurations: Connected Meta WhatsApp Business phone numbers, Facebook Page IDs, Instagram handles, SMS gateway keys, and SMTP credentials necessary to deliver omnichannel routing services.
  • Knowledge Base Data: Document uploads, PDFs, FAQs, and web URLs that you choose to submit to train your custom AI chatbot knowledge bases.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and interaction logs collected via essential session cookies and analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes:

  • Delivering, provisioning, and maintaining the BotifyAI platform and mobile applications.
  • Processing customer transactions, authenticating user licenses, and providing customer support.
  • Routing and processing automated customer communications across connected Meta, SMS, and email channels.
  • Ensuring security, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance.

4. Data Controller & GDPR Compliance (EU / UK Users)

For individuals residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, FinxHost Ltd acts as a Data Controller for account data and a Data Processor for customer conversation data managed within your account. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you hold the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data when no longer needed for lawful purposes.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Restrict Processing & Withdraw Consent: Object to or restrict specific processing operations at any time.

5. Payment Security & Third-Party Processing

All financial transactions are securely processed through JVZoo and authorized payment gateways (including PayPal and Stripe). BotifyAI never stores or has direct access to your credit card numbers or banking credentials.

6. Data Retention & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards (including TLS/SSL encryption, secure API endpoints, and role-based access controls) to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill service delivery and legal obligations.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes standard cookies to ensure website functionality, save user preferences, and measure traffic analytics. For detailed information on cookie usage and management, please see our Cookie Policy.
