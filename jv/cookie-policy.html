Cookie Policy

Information regarding how BotifyAI uses cookies and similar tracking technologies

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a smoother browsing experience, and deliver reporting information to site owners.

2. How BotifyAI Uses Cookies

BotifyAI (operated by FinxHost Ltd) uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential & Operational Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website and platform to function properly, including user authentication, session security, and load balancing.
  • Affiliate Tracking & Analytics Cookies: When visitors arrive via JVZoo affiliate links, secure cookies ensure accurate referral attribution and checkout routing.
  • Performance & Analytics: We use analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel) to analyze aggregate visitor traffic patterns, page load speeds, and user navigation paths to continually improve our platform.
  • User Preferences: These cookies remember your display settings, language preferences, and interface customizations.

3. Managing and Disabling Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control and manage cookie settings through browser preferences. You can configure your browser to reject all cookies, alert you when a cookie is placed, or delete existing cookies from your device.

Please note that disabling certain essential cookies may impact the functionality of our website, login portals, or checkout forms.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our site are set by third-party services, including JVZoo payment infrastructure, Google Ads, and Meta Analytics. We do not control these third-party cookies directly; please consult the respective privacy policies of these third-party providers for further details.
