BotifyAI Anti-Spam Policy

  • At BotifyAI, we maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards unsolicited messaging and spam across all supported communication channels, including WhatsApp, SMS, Email, Messenger, and Instagram. By creating an account and using our platform, you agree to strictly comply with this Anti-Spam Policy. Channel Compliance Guidelines: 1. Email: All outbound broadcast emails sent through BotifyAI or connected SMTP servers must comply with the CAN-SPAM Act of 2003 and applicable regional regulations (including GDPR). Every marketing email must include a clear, working one-click unsubscribe link and valid sender contact information. 2. WhatsApp: All outbound broadcasts sent via the WhatsApp Business Cloud API must utilize Meta-approved message templates and adhere to Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging and Commerce Policies. You must obtain verifiable opt-in consent from recipients prior to sending marketing templates. 3. SMS: All text messaging must comply with TCPA, CTIA guidelines, and local telecommunications regulations. Unsolicited marketing SMS is strictly prohibited, and recipients must have a clear mechanism to opt out (e.g., replying STOP). Account Termination: If BotifyAI determines that an account is sending spam, engaging in abusive broadcasting, or receiving high spam complaint/block rates from carriers or Meta, we reserve the right to immediately suspend or terminate access without refund. If you suspect that our platform is being used to transmit spam, please report it immediately to our support team at support@finxhost.com.
