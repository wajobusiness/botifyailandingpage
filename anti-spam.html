Anti-Spam & Acceptable Messaging Policy

CAN-SPAM, TCPA, GDPR & Meta WhatsApp Messaging Compliance

1. Zero Tolerance for Spam

BotifyAI (operated by FinxHost Ltd) maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding unsolicited commercial communications ("spam"). You may not use BotifyAI, its omnichannel routing engine, SMS gateways, email dispatchers, or Meta WhatsApp Business API integrations to send unsolicited, deceptive, or abusive messages of any kind.

2. Explicit Consent & Opt-In Requirements

You agree to only initiate communications with individuals who have provided explicit, verifiable, opt-in consent to receive communications from your organization:

  • WhatsApp Business Messaging: All proactive business-initiated messages must use Meta-approved message templates and be delivered solely to recipients who have opted in under Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.
  • SMS & MMS Marketing: You must comply with all applicable telecommunications regulations (including TCPA in the US and CTIA standards), ensuring clear opt-in and immediate handling of STOP/unsubscribe requests.
  • Email Broadcasting: All outgoing marketing emails must comply with the US CAN-SPAM Act, GDPR, and CASL, including accurate sender header information, clear identification of commercial intent, a valid physical mailing address, and a functional 1-click unsubscribe link.

3. Prohibited Activities

Users of BotifyAI are strictly prohibited from:

  • Using scraped, rented, or purchased contact lists without verifiable opt-in records.
  • Sending deceptive subject lines, misleading sender identities, or falsified sender headers.
  • Promoting illegal goods, counterfeit products, adult content, deceptive financial schemes, or prohibited categories under Meta's Commerce Policy.
  • Harassing, threatening, or impersonating individuals or companies.

4. Enforcement & Account Termination

We actively monitor abuse reports. If a user is found to be violating this Anti-Spam Policy or triggering excessive spam complaints, FinxHost Ltd reserves the right to immediately suspend or permanently terminate account access without refund and report abusive conduct to relevant gateway authorities.

If you have received an unwanted message generated through a BotifyAI account or have questions regarding our messaging standards, please report it immediately to our compliance department at:
